# Privacy Policy — PDF Form Filler & Signer

**Last updated: May 26, 2026**

## Overview

PDF Form Filler & Signer is a browser-based tool that lets you fill PDF forms, draw signatures, and add text annotations. Your privacy is fully protected: the extension collects absolutely no data.

## Data Collection

**We collect nothing.** This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or analytics of any kind.

## How the Extension Works

All processing happens entirely within your browser:

- PDF files you open are loaded and rendered locally on your device.
- Signatures, form fields, and annotations you add never leave your browser.
- The finished document is downloaded directly to your device.
- No data is sent to any server, third party, or remote service at any point.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following browser permissions:

- **storage** — used solely to save your uploaded signatures locally on your device.

This permission is not used to collect or transmit any data.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party service, analytics platform, or advertising network.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published with a new "Last updated" date. Given that no data is collected, no material changes are anticipated.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the extension's repository or contact the developer directly.
